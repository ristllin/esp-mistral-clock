# Mistral AI pixel-M logo grid

Traced from the official 2025 Mistral AI logo SVG
(Wikimedia Commons: `Mistral_AI_logo_(2025-).svg`, (c) Mistral AI).
`tools/gen_pixel_art.py` renders `src/assets/mistral_logo.h` from this grid.

Grid: 7 columns x 5 rows, `X` = filled cell. Colours are the official palette.

```
col:   0 1 2 3 4 5 6
Row 0: . X . . . X .   #FFD800  yellow
Row 1: . X X . X X .   #FFAF00  amber
Row 2: . X X X X X .   #FF8205  orange
Row 3: . X . X . X .   #FA500F  red-orange
Row 4: X X X . X X X   #E10500  red
```
